## Measurement limits

Native asynchronous save can overlap writing with training. Its writing elapsed measurement includes that overlap, so phase totals must not be added to wall time. These runs use a local CPU filesystem; they do not measure GPU, multi-node, storage-delay, disk-loss, or host-power-failure behavior. This four-step workload is short and includes launch overhead; three repetitions cannot establish a general performance advantage.

## Reproduce
